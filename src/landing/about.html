Who we are

Built to make the journey simpler.

Finding the right place to stay should be simple, transparent, and convenient. Whether you are planning a weekend getaway, travelling for business, relocating to a new city, or searching for somewhere to call home, SuiteMonger brings accommodation options and essential services together in one accessible platform.

SuiteMonger is a technology-driven platform developed by Alphahoop Limited, a registered Nigerian company committed to building practical digital solutions that make everyday experiences more connected and convenient.

We recognised a common challenge: customers often navigate multiple platforms, contact different agents, compare unclear prices, and manage arrangements through disconnected channels. Property owners and service professionals face the same fragmentation from the other side.

Because finding somewhere to stay should not be the difficult part of your journey.

Our purpose

A clearer way to find, offer, and access what people need.

01 / Mission

Simplify every connection.

To simplify accommodation discovery and booking while connecting people with the spaces and services they need through a convenient, transparent, and accessible digital platform.

We aim to help customers find suitable accommodation, give property owners tools to reach more people, and create opportunities for service providers to grow their businesses.

02 / Vision

One integrated marketplace.

To become a leading integrated accommodation and services marketplace, making it easier for people to find spaces, access services, and connect with opportunities across Nigeria and beyond.

We envision a single digital experience that works wherever a customer is searching from.

What we do

Three parts of the experience.
One organised marketplace.

SuiteMonger connects accommodation discovery, property listing, and professional services while keeping each journey clear.

01

Accommodation discovery & booking

Explore available properties, review accommodation details, compare pricing, and submit booking requests directly through the platform.

  • Short-let and serviced apartments
  • Hotels and hospitality properties
  • Residential and longer-stay accommodation
  • Other eligible properties from approved listers
02

Property listing & management

Approved property owners, managers, and accommodation businesses can showcase their spaces and connect with prospective guests.

  • Booking and transaction records
03

Service-provider discovery

Eligible independent professionals can present their services and connect with customers who need help before, during, or after a stay.

  • Professional service profiles
  • Customer discovery
  • Service-area information
  • New opportunities to grow

How SuiteMonger works

A focused path for every participant.

For guests & customers

  1. 01
    Explore freely

    Browse public listings, details, prices, and available service categories without an account.

  2. 02
    Find what suits you

    Compare information, location, pricing, accommodation options, and availability.

  3. 03
    Book with confidence

    Create an account, select your details, and complete payment to submit a request.

  4. 04
    Receive confirmation

    Your booking is confirmed when the property lister approves the request.

For property listers

  1. 01
    Create your account

    Register as a property lister through the SuiteMonger mobile application.

  2. 02
    Complete your profile

    Provide the required information and submit your profile for review.

  3. 03
    Get approved

    Begin listing eligible properties with relevant details after approval.

  4. 04
    Connect with customers

    Receive and manage requests from customers interested in your accommodation.

For service providers

Turn expertise into discoverable opportunity.

Service professionals can join SuiteMonger, complete the necessary registration and approval requirements, and showcase their services to potential customers.

Our aim is to make customer discovery more accessible while supporting a more connected service marketplace.

Built around customer confidence

A booking process designed for clarity.

Customers should know what has happened, what comes next, and what every amount represents.

24hours for the lister to respond

Payment starts the request. Approval confirms the booking.

When a guest completes payment, the payment is held while the lister reviews the request. If the lister does not confirm within 24 hours, the request expires and the accommodation booking payment is refunded, excluding the applicable SuiteMonger service charge.

Clear pricing

Know what builds the total.

The listed rate, number of suites or units, stay duration, and applicable service charges are presented before payment.

Flexible payments

Options shown at checkout.

Supported local and international methods may vary by location, currency, and payment-provider availability.
