Status should never be a mystery

Know what is happening
after every action.

SuiteMonger distinguishes a request from a confirmed booking and a submitted payment from a completed one. Every state reflects the connected backend.

Request sent

The lister or provider still needs to review your request.

Confirmed

The connected booking system has confirmed the reservation or appointment.

Payment failed

No success state is shown. Details remain available so the customer can recover.

Cancelled

The record remains visible with the applicable cancellation and refund information.

Visit help & support
Can I explore SuiteMonger without an account?

Yes! You can freely explore SuiteMonger without creating an account. Browse available properties, view apartment details, compare prices, and discover services offered by providers. You’ll only need to create an account when you’re ready to make a booking, save your favourites, or complete a transaction.

Can I explore as a guest in the mobile app?

Absolutely! SuiteMonger supports guest browsing on both Android and iOS. Simply download the app from Google Play or the Apple App Store and explore available properties and services without signing up. When you’re ready to book or access personalised features, you can create an account in just a few steps.

When is a booking confirmed?

Your booking request is initiated once payment is successfully completed. For your protection, your payment is securely held while the property lister reviews and approves your request. The lister has up to 24 hours to confirm the booking. If they fail to approve it within this period, the request automatically expires, and you’ll receive a full refund of your booking payment, excluding the SuiteMonger service charge. Your booking is officially confirmed only after the lister approves it.

Please note: A successful payment is not the same as a confirmed booking. The SuiteMonger service charge is non-refundable if the lister does not respond within 24 hours; this condition is also shown before checkout.

How are prices presented?

SuiteMonger calculates your booking cost based on the property’s listed rate, the number of suites or units selected, and the duration of your stay. Applicable SuiteMonger service charges are then added to determine your total booking amount. You’ll see a clear breakdown of your costs before making payment, so you know exactly what you’re paying for.

How do I list a property or offer a service?

Getting started as a lister or service provider on SuiteMonger is simple! Download the SuiteMonger app from Google Play or the Apple App Store, create an account, and complete your profile with the required information. Once your profile has been reviewed and approved, you’re ready to list your properties or offer your services and connect with potential customers.

Which payment methods are supported?

SuiteMonger supports a wide range of local and international payment options, making it convenient for customers to book from Nigeria and around the world. Available methods may include debit and credit cards, bank transfers, and other supported payment channels, depending on your location and payment provider. All available options will be displayed securely at checkout.
